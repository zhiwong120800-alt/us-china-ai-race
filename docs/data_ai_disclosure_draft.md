# Data and AI disclosure — draft (must be reviewed by entire group)

## Data
The project uses documented frozen extracts of Epoch AI's Notable AI Models dataset and Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI) plus AI publication statistics produced by CSET and redistributed by Our World in Data. Full source citations, collection dates and license notes: `docs/data_sources_and_licenses.md`.

## AI assistance
Members should record every substantive AI use in `docs/ai_provenance_template.csv` or their contribution log. The final disclosure must reflect actual AI use by each member and explicitly distinguish accepted, modified and rejected outputs. Do not submit this draft as a completed statement before validating the team's actual usage.
