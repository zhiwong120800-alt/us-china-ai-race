Methods & limitations

What each category means

Open weights: Epoch labels indicating downloadable model weights, including restricted-use and non-commercial variants. This does not necessarily mean open-source code, training data or unrestricted reuse.

API / hosted: an API or hosted interface with no downloadable weights indicated by the source field. Unreleased: Epoch's unreleased label. Unknown: missing or unrecognised source labels, including limited access.

The labels are taken from the source's release accessibility field, not inferred from a model's perceived openness.

What this cannot establish

Epoch's Notable AI Models is a curated sample, not a census. The selection of notable models changes over time, and early years have a substantial number of missing accessibility labels.

Multiple-country developer credits are whole-counted; the same model can appear in both national totals. Developer type follows Chart 4's mutually exclusive taxonomy, including its unresolved Other/Unknown classifications.

Release accessibility measures a distribution strategy. It does not by itself measure safety, model quality, training transparency or commercial success.
