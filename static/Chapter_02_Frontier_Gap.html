What does research say about the compute gap?

Our evidence: The line tracks the largest reported training-compute estimate available for each country and date, not every frontier model and not a direct test of model quality.

Research context: Sevilla and colleagues document the strong historical rise in machine-learning training compute. DeepSeek-V3's technical report illustrates that architecture and training choices also matter. These sources motivate studying compute without treating our observed US–China FLOP ratio as an equivalent performance ratio.
