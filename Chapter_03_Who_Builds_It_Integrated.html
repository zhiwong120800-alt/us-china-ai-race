Participation isn't control.

Epoch’s Notable AI Models dataset is a curated selection, not a census of every model. More records attached to an organisation do not prove better model performance, greater influence, or independent creation.

There are 667 country–model assignments for 2016–2025, with 514 in the US and 153 in China. Joint US–China models can appear in both totals. A model involving several labs can appear in several ranked bars.

How this ranking was built

Source: Epoch AI · Notable AI Models, downloaded 20 October 2026. Publication dates cover 2016–2025.

Developer attribution: Organisation and country labels are paired when the source’s list lengths agree. For ambiguous rows, the original processing uses other aligned Epoch rows plus nine individually documented country overrides; one organisation remains unassigned. See the public chart3_org_map.csv for every decision.

Alias mode: The audited parent-company view groups related labs and rebrands (Google, Meta, Alibaba, Huawei and others), but preserves distinct joint labs and MSR Asia in China. This is an explicitly defined attribution choice, not a ranking of company quality. The exact original-label view remains available.

Top-three share: the number of distinct country–model records involving at least one of the three highest-ranked developers divided by all country–model records in the selected period. It is not the sum of the top three bars.

Reproduce: python scripts/build_chart3.py using the group’s 1,078-row frozen Epoch file. The source supplied by Manasvi contains one additional 2026 entry; 2016–2025 results remain unchanged. Hong Kong: kept separate by default, with an optional alternative (China 153 → 157 distinct models) that does not modify the other chapters.
